About Us

Discover our story and services

Welcome to Hager Bet

Hager Bet is more than just a restaurant – it's a cultural home. Inspired by the warmth of Ethiopian and Eritrean traditions, we bring people together through food, music, and community. From the aroma of freshly roasted coffee to the sharing of injera, every detail tells the story of our heritage.

Established with love and respect for our roots, Hager Bet is where tradition meets modern hospitality.

Our Services

At Hager Bet, we provide a full cultural dining experience. From traditional Ethiopian meals to warm coffee ceremonies, we are committed to authenticity and quality.

  • ☕ Traditional Coffee Ceremony
